## Proposed niche
**Human-centered intelligent digital systems.**
Focus: exploring software, applied intelligence and dependable digital infrastructure to simplify complicated experiences for people and organizations. This is a positioning direction, not a claim of available enterprise services.

### Brand proposition
**Headline:** Technology that makes complexity useful.
**Alternative:** Technology built around real needs. (current approved copy; retain unless new wording approved)
**Supporting copy:** Pectrium explores how software, intelligent systems and digital infrastructure can help people and organizations navigate complex tasks with greater clarity.
**Distinction:** problem-to-system design across digital experiences, intelligence, data and infrastructure. Do not promise products, sectors, customer results or global reach not yet demonstrated.

## Proposed visual signature: Human Systems in Motion
Original commissioned art direction rather than Mercuryo's yacht/boardwalk:
- Aerial perspective of an **urban interchange of human-scale paths and connected civic/commercial spaces**, with small groups of people walking, working and interacting in different zones.
- Architectural ribbons represent connections among people, services and information, but should not literally reproduce Mercuryo's curved boardwalk, boat or waterfront framing.
- Natural photographic realism, deep neutral graphite, warm stone, minimal muted blue-grey. Black/white UI remains the dominant identity; no borrowed turquoise/blue-glow buttons.
- Desktop: image carries the right two-thirds with clear negative space for short left-side copy. Mobile: intentional 4:5 crop with visible human activity and legible type panel below.
- Text and CTA in DOM, never baked into a background image; provide accessible alt for informative photography; reduced-motion fallback.
- If an animated treatment is proposed later, use slow parallax or ambient motion only after performance/accessibility tests, with `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback and static poster.

## Proposed homepage editorial system
1. **Identity / hero:** precise company positioning; original Human Systems in Motion visual; CTA to Solutions, not a fake transactional CTA.
2. **What we're exploring:** problem-oriented solution directions: digital workflows, intelligent assistance, connected systems, data clarity. Explicit R&D labels.
3. **Where ideas take shape:** authentic, verified examples or annotated concept studies; no invented product UI or case studies.
4. **Technology behind the work:** application engineering, AI, cloud and data, separate from Solutions.
5. **Initiatives:** Quincestone, Emperor Armani, Clesla only; stage, relationship and descriptions require review.
6. **Evidence, not invented metrics:** only substantiated milestones with sources; until available, replace numbers and customer logos with working principles, research process or published documentation.
7. **People and company:** genuine founder/company information once approved; no invented team photos, staff counts, offices or jobs.
8. **Insights and next step:** published writing if available; verified contact channel when ready. Avoid dead-end CTA.

## Category distinction from Mercuryo
Mercuryo: web3 payment infrastructure, on-ramp/off-ramp, crypto integrations and commercial transaction flows.
Pectrium proposal: technology company exploring **human-centered intelligent digital systems** spanning software experiences, applied intelligence and infrastructure. Not a crypto company, payment processor, wallet, exchange, IT support firm, or generic web agency.
